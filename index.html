The Explorer

Driven by the
call of wild places

I'm Claviston Souza — an explorer, adventurer, and landscape enthusiast with a deep passion for the raw, untamed beauty of the Americas. From the crimson spires of Sedona to the towering canyon walls of Zion, every expedition is a journey into the extraordinary.

With backpack in hand and horizon in sight, I've spent years seeking out the most breathtaking landscapes this continent has to offer. These aren't just photographs — they're memories carved in stone, light, and sky.
